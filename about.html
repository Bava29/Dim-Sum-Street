OUR STORY

Where Our Story Begins

A love for traditional dim sum, good food and bringing people together is at the heart of Dim Sum Street.

Tradition Served With Heart

Dim Sum Street was created with a simple idea — to bring the warmth and spirit of traditional Chinese dim sum to a welcoming modern table.

Inspired by the lively streets, tea houses and timeless food traditions of China, our restaurant is a place where handcrafted baskets and freshly brewed tea come together with good company.

Every dish is prepared with care, using thoughtfully selected ingredients and traditional techniques. We believe that great food tastes even better when it is shared.
