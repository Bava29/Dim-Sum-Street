GOOD TO KNOW

Your Brunch Questions, Answered.

Do I need to reserve a table?

We recommend reserving ahead, especially for weekend brunch and larger groups. Use our booking page to request your table.

Can I visit with a group?

Absolutely! Our sharing-style brunch is ideal for friends and family. Please include your group size when making a reservation.

Are vegetarian options available?

Vegetarian choices can be requested. Please check the current menu and mention any dietary requirements when booking.

Can I request a special occasion table?

Let our team know about birthdays, celebrations or seating preferences when you submit your reservation request.

What if I arrive late?

If you expect to be late, contact the restaurant as soon as possible. Table availability may depend on the day's bookings.
